# Terms and Privacy drafts — 22 October 2026

The user requested review drafts. `src/PolicyDrafts.tsx` contains the proposed text displayed through Help → Terms of Service / Privacy Policy. Both pages are labeled draft, have no effective date and do not collect acceptance. Opening the pages does not constitute consent. They are available to signed-in roles, including accounts without a vehicle, and survive refresh through the existing page-restoration mechanism.

Text was grounded in account roles/assignments, session/storage handling, GPS/telemetry, contact/SMS preferences, provider configuration and the account lifecycle/retention tools. The draft does not promise a production host, automatic scheduled cleanup, emergency response, SMS delivery, blanket erasure, ISO compliance or security guarantees. It distinguishes SMS consent from other processing purposes.

Before approval, confirm:

- Responsible organization/controller name and privacy/service contact details.
- Actual hosting/services, recipients, access/export permissions and international processing.
- Lawful basis and necessity for each use, including location and any research processing.
- Retention/disposal periods or criteria for each record category and backups; whether cleanup is actually scheduled.
- Rights-request, complaint, incident and policy-change procedures.
- Terms covering eligibility, responsibilities, service/access changes and applicable legal provisions, reviewed by the responsible organization/adviser.
- Final version, approval and effective date.

Research consent and approved study procedures are separate from these draft pages. Any future use of the pages during signup requires separately approved wording and acceptance/notice behavior; no signup or backend contract was changed here.

Privacy-notice structure reference: [National Privacy Commission — the right to be informed](https://privacy.gov.ph/the-right-to-be-informed/). The source is guidance, not approval of SOLAIR or its draft text.
