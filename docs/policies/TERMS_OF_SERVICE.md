## 4. Reported information and safe use

Interact with the app only when it is safe to do so. Stop the vehicle before using controls that would distract you from driving. Follow applicable road-safety requirements and your TODA's procedures.

Readings depend on device configuration, calibration, connectivity and available records. Information may be missing, delayed, historical or simulated. A recent device connection does not establish that every sensor is current. Battery voltage is not a remaining-charge percentage or driving range. An illustration is not evidence of installed or commissioned hardware.

A city reference is not a reported vehicle position. Location history, detected stops and point-to-point distances are estimates derived from available GPS records; they do not independently establish an exact address, route, visit, arrival or departure. Graph gaps are not proof that a vehicle stopped or a device was disconnected.

A displayed security state does not independently establish that a vehicle is safe, that a siren sounded or that an incident was resolved. SOLAIR does not replace physical inspection, professional maintenance or the responsible operator's assessment.

## 5. Assistance requests, messages and notifications

Assistance requests and messages depend on network and backend availability. QUEUED / UNSENT means server receipt was not confirmed. Server received means the server accepted the request; it does not mean an operator has acknowledged it or that help has been dispatched. Check recorded request activity and use your established TODA contact arrangements when follow-up is needed.

SOLAIR is not an emergency-dispatch service. Do not rely on it as your sole channel for urgent assistance. An Emergency SOS selection does not establish that police, medical responders or other outside services have been contacted.

Reading a notification or selecting Mark all read changes the notification's read state. It does not acknowledge, resolve or close a security incident. In-app badge preferences are separate from browser push and SMS settings.
